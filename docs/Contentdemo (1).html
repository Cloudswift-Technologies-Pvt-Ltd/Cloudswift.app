// 02 — the problem

Why AI SaaS products stall before launch

Most founders don't fail at the idea. They fail at the assembly problem — four hard engineering disciplines, needed at once, before there's revenue to justify four hires.

Hiring

The full-stack team problem

LLM engineer, cloud architect, security engineer, and product team — simultaneously, pre-revenue. Most teams end up with one generalist covering all four badly.

Architecture

The multi-tenancy trap

Build single-tenant first, "add multi-tenancy later" — and most teams re-architect the entire data layer right when growth should be accelerating, not stalling.

Economics

The inference cost surprise

LLM costs that look fine in a demo don't scale linearly. Teams without cost controls discover this after a pricing plan is already locked in with customers.

Compliance

The audit afterthought

Security and SOC 2 readiness get bolted on right before a big enterprise deal needs them — under deadline pressure, fighting the architecture instead of supporting it.

// 03 — definition

What is AI SaaS product development?

The end-to-end engineering of a commercial, subscription-based product with an LLM or predictive ML model as a core part of its functionality — spanning five layers that need to be designed together, not sequentially:

01

Application layer

Product UI, UX, and business logic.

02

AI / model layer

LLM integration, RAG pipelines, predictive ML models.

03

Multi-tenant infrastructure

One deployment serving many customers with strict data isolation.

04

Commerce layer

Subscription billing, metering, usage-based pricing.

05

Security & compliance layer

Audit-readiness and data isolation guarantees for enterprise buyers.

// 13 — questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build an AI SaaS product to launch?

From product requirements to a commercially launchable v1, plan for 4 to 8 months depending on scope. We typically ship an internal alpha around the midpoint so design partners can validate direction before launch hardening.

What does multi-tenant architecture mean and why does it matter for AI SaaS?

Multi-tenancy lets one deployment serve many customers with strict data isolation. For AI products, tenant data must be isolated in vector stores and model contexts too, so one customer's documents never leak into another's answers.

Can you handle the AI model layer as well as the application?

Yes — that's the point of an integrated team. We implement LLM features via Azure OpenAI or comparable APIs, build RAG pipelines, add predictive ML models, and wrap everything with evaluation and cost controls.

Do you work with funded startups in India as well as US companies?

Yes. Indian startups work directly with local engineering leadership in Bengaluru and Mumbai. US companies contract through our Delaware entity, with architecture decisions handled in US-friendly hours.

How do you control LLM inference costs as the product scales?

Cost monitoring and evaluation pipelines are built alongside the AI features from day one — tracking cost-per-tenant and cost-per-feature before a pricing plan is locked in, including model-selection tradeoffs and caching for repeated query patterns.
